The Genesis

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. As the eldest of nine siblings and son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (renowned Neurosurgeon), Mumtaz's journey began in the humblest of settings. From village schooling to scholarship-based excellence at Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools, his path has been illuminated by discipline, brilliance, and an unwavering commitment to justice.

2019 - The Foundation

Graduated from Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges with distinction. Demonstrated exceptional leadership, multilingual fluency (Pashto, Urdu, English, Turkish), and laid the groundwork for legal excellence.

2022 - The Awakening

Earned BA (Arts) from University of Balochistan. Built the intellectual foundation for social sciences, political philosophy, and human rights advocacy that would define his career.

2024 - The Transformation

Dual achievement: LLB (Hons) from University Law College and MA Political Science from University of Balochistan. Mastered the intricate dance between law and governance, theory and practice.

2025 - The Ascent

Received Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025). Formally joined Iqra Law Associates under the mentorship of Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar. Began formal practice as Advocate High Court.
