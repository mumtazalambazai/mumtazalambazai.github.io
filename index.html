The Journey

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. Mumtaz is the eldest of nine siblings, son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon). From village schooling to scholarship-based excellence at Pak-Turk Maarif, his journey is defined by discipline and brilliance. Fluent in Pashto, Urdu, English, and Turkish, he represents the new generation of Pakistani leaders combining traditional values with global perspectives.

2019 - Intermediate Education

Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges. Demonstrated exceptional leadership skills and multilingual fluency (Pashto, Urdu, English, Turkish). Received merit-based scholarship for outstanding academic performance.

2022 - BA (Arts) Degree

University of Balochistan. Built strong foundation in social sciences, political thought, and humanities. Actively participated in student leadership and community service initiatives.

2024 - LLB (Hons) & MA Political Science

University Law College & University of Balochistan. Graduated with honors, mastering the intricacies of law and governance. Published research on electoral systems and human rights.

2025 - Legal License & Practice

Received Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025). Formally joined Iqra Law Associates under Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar. Began active practice in Civil, Criminal, and Human Rights litigation.
