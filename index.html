About Me

Personal Background

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. Eldest of nine siblings, son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon). My journey from village schooling to becoming an Advocate High Court reflects discipline, resilience, and commitment to justice.

Mission & Vision

To advance justice, safeguard human dignity, strengthen democratic institutions, and empower youth to lead Pakistan toward a just, inclusive, and sustainable future through legal excellence and compassionate leadership.

Legal Philosophy

I believe in law as an instrument of social change. My practice combines rigorous legal expertise with deep empathy for marginalized communities, ensuring access to justice for all, regardless of socioeconomic status.

Education & Journey

2019 - Intermediate (F.Sc Pre-Engineering)

Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges. Demonstrated exceptional leadership skills and multilingual fluency (Pashto, Urdu, English, Turkish). Awarded scholarship for academic excellence.

2022 - BA (Arts)

University of Balochistan. Built a strong foundation in social sciences, political thought, and humanities while actively participating in student leadership and community service.

2024 - LLB (Hons) & MA Political Science

University Law College & University of Balochistan. Graduated with honors, mastering intricate aspects of constitutional law, human rights, and governance. Authored research papers on electoral reforms.

2025 - Advocate High Court License

Received Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025). Formally joined Iqra Law Associates under mentorship of Senior Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar. Specialized in civil, criminal, and human rights litigation.
