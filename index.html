The Journey of Excellence

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan, Mumtaz Alam Bazai is the eldest of nine siblings and son of renowned neurosurgeon Dr. Ameer Muhammad. His journey from humble village schooling to becoming a globally recognized legal advocate exemplifies discipline, brilliance, and unwavering commitment to justice.

2019 - Intermediate Excellence

Graduated from Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges with distinction, demonstrating exceptional leadership and multilingual fluency in Pashto, Urdu, English, and Turkish. Active in debate, Model UN, and community service initiatives.

2022 - BA (Arts) Degree

Completed Bachelor of Arts from University of Balochistan, building a strong foundation in social sciences, political thought, and humanities that would inform his legal and advocacy work.

2024 - Dual Academic Achievement

Graduated with honors with an LLB (Hons) from University Law College and MA in Political Science from University of Balochistan, mastering the intricate relationship between law, governance, and societal change.

2025 - Legal License & Practice

Received Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025) and formally joined Iqra Law Associates under the mentorship of Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar, embarking on a professional legal career dedicated to justice.

Legal & Social Impact

Legal Career

Active practitioner in Civil, Criminal, and Human Rights litigation at the High Court level. Known for extensive pro bono work for marginalized communities in Balochistan, providing legal aid to those who cannot afford representation. Trained at prestigious Iqra Law Associates in Quetta.

Global Shaper (WEF)

Curator of Quetta Hub at the World Economic Forum's Global Shapers Community. Represented Pakistan at the Global Shapers Annual Summit 2025 in Geneva. Authored the Governance Charter and spearheaded Pakistan's first comprehensive Youth Policy Project.

Youth Policy Development International Diplomacy Community Leadership Global Networking

Publications & Research

Author of "Human Rights and Responsibilities" (2021), a comprehensive guide to fundamental rights. Conducted extensive research on "The Role of ECP in Free and Fair Elections" (2024). Regular contributor to legal journals and policy papers on governance and human rights.
