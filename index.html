The Journey

From humble beginnings in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, to becoming a Global Shaper at the World Economic Forum

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. Mumtaz is the eldest of nine siblings, son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon). His journey from village schooling to scholarship-based excellence at Pak-Turk Maarif is defined by discipline, brilliance, and unwavering commitment to justice.
