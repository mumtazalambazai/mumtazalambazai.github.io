Biography

The Journey of Excellence

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. Mumtaz Alam Bazai is the eldest of nine siblings, son of renowned Neurosurgeon Dr. Ameer Muhammad. His journey from village schooling to becoming a distinguished legal professional embodies resilience, dedication, and unwavering commitment to justice.

Academic Excellence

Completed early education with distinction at Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges, demonstrating exceptional leadership skills and multilingual fluency in Pashto, Urdu, English, and Turkish.

Higher Education

Earned BA (Arts) from University of Balochistan (2022), followed by LLB (Hons) from University Law College and MA Political Science from University of Balochistan (2024), graduating with honors.

Legal Career

Received Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025) and joined Iqra Law Associates under esteemed Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar, establishing a reputation for excellence in legal practice.
