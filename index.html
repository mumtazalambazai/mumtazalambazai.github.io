About Me

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. Mumtaz is the eldest of nine siblings, son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon).

Legal Visionary & Human Rights Advocate

From village schooling to scholarship-based excellence at Pak-Turk Maarif, my journey is defined by discipline and brilliance. As a practicing lawyer, Global Shaper, and community leader, I am committed to advancing justice and empowering youth in Balochistan and beyond.

My legal practice focuses on civil, criminal, and human rights litigation, with a strong emphasis on pro bono work for marginalized communities. As Curator of the Quetta Hub for Global Shapers (World Economic Forum), I represent Balochistan at international forums and lead youth empowerment initiatives.

  • Advocate High Court with Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025)
  • Global Shaper at World Economic Forum, Curator of Quetta Hub
  • LLB (Hons) & MA Political Science Graduate
  • Author of "Human Rights and Responsibilities" (2021)
  • Fluent in Pashto, Urdu, English, and Turkish
