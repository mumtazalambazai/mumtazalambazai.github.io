About Me

From the mountains of Balochistan to global platforms, my journey is a testament to perseverance, excellence, and commitment to justice.

Education

LL.B (Hons) from University Law College, MA Political Science from University of Balochistan, BA Arts from University of Balochistan, Intermediate from Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges.

Background

Born on August 11, 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. Eldest of nine siblings, son of renowned neurosurgeon Dr. Ameer Muhammad. Fluent in Pashto, Urdu, English, and Turkish.

Mission

To advance justice, safeguard human dignity, strengthen democratic institutions, and empower youth to lead Pakistan toward a just, inclusive, and sustainable future.

Legal Career & Achievements

A track record of excellence in legal practice, human rights advocacy, and global leadership.

2025 - Advocate High Court

Officially licensed as Advocate High Court (Reg No. 14/2025). Joined Iqra Law Associates under mentorship of Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar. Specializing in Civil, Criminal, and Constitutional Law.

2024 - Global Shaper Curator

Appointed as Curator of Quetta Hub, World Economic Forum's Global Shapers Community. Represented Pakistan at Global Shapers Annual Summit 2025 in Geneva, Switzerland.

2023 - Youth Policy Project

Spearheaded the first Youth Policy Project for Balochistan. Authored the Governance Charter and presented policy recommendations to provincial authorities.

2022 - Legal Research & Publications

Published "Human Rights and Responsibilities" (2021) and research paper on "The Role of Election Commission of Pakistan in Free and Fair Elections" (2024).

2021 - Pro Bono Initiatives

Founded legal aid program for marginalized communities in Balochistan. Provided free legal assistance to over 200 individuals from disadvantaged backgrounds.
