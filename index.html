The Journey

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. Mumtaz is the eldest of nine siblings, son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon). From village schooling to scholarship-based excellence at Pak-Turk Maarif, his journey is defined by discipline, brilliance, and an unwavering commitment to justice.

2019 - Intermediate

Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges. Demonstrated exceptional leadership and multilingual fluency (Pashto, Urdu, English, Turkish). Awarded multiple academic scholarships.

2022 - BA (Arts)

University of Balochistan. Building the foundation for social sciences and political thought. Active in student leadership and community development initiatives.

2024 - LLB (Hons) & MA Pol. Sci.

University Law College & University of Balochistan. Graduated with honors, mastering the intricacies of law and governance. Published research on electoral reforms.

2025 - Legal License

Received Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025). Formally joined Iqra Law Associates under Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar. Began practice in Civil, Criminal, and Human Rights litigation.

Legal & Social Impact

Legal Career

Active practitioner in Civil, Criminal, and Human Rights litigation. Known for pro bono work for marginalized communities in Balochistan. Trained at Iqra Law Associates, Quetta.

Global Shaper (WEF)

Publications
