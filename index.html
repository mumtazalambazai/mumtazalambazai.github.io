The Journey

From humble beginnings to becoming a voice for justice and change

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. Mumtaz is the eldest of nine siblings, son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon). From village schooling to scholarship-based excellence at Pak-Turk Maarif, his journey is defined by discipline, brilliance, and unwavering commitment to justice.

2019 - Intermediate

Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges. Demonstrated exceptional leadership and multilingual fluency (Pashto, Urdu, English, Turkish).

2022 - BA (Arts)

University of Balochistan. Building the foundation for social sciences and political thought while actively engaging in student leadership.

2024 - LLB (Hons) & MA Pol. Sci.

University Law College & University of Balochistan. Graduated with honors, mastering the intricacies of law and governance while contributing to legal research.

2025 - Legal License

Received Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025). Formally joined Iqra Law Associates under the mentorship of Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar.

Legal & Social Impact

Bridging law, leadership, and human rights advocacy

Legal Career

Active practitioner in Civil, Criminal, and Human Rights litigation. Known for extensive pro bono work for marginalized communities in Balochistan. Trained at Iqra Law Associates, Quetta, with a focus on constitutional law.

Global Shaper (WEF)

Curator of Quetta Hub. Represented at Global Shapers Annual Summit 2025 (Geneva). Authored the Governance Charter and spearheaded the first Youth Policy Project for Balochistan's sustainable development.

Publications & Research

Author of "Human Rights and Responsibilities" (2021) and groundbreaking research on "The Role of ECP in Free and Fair Elections" (2024). Regular contributor to legal journals and policy papers.
