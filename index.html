The Quantum Journey

From the valleys of Balochistan to global platforms, a journey of resilience, excellence, and transformative leadership.

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. Mumtaz is the eldest of nine siblings, son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon). From village schooling to scholarship-based excellence at Pak-Turk Maarif, his journey is defined by discipline, brilliance, and an unwavering commitment to justice.

2019 - Intermediate Excellence

Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges. Demonstrated exceptional leadership, multilingual fluency (Pashto, Urdu, English, Turkish), and academic brilliance that set the foundation for future achievements.

2022 - BA (Arts) Foundation

University of Balochistan. Built a strong foundation in social sciences and political thought, while actively engaging in student leadership and community development initiatives.

2024 - Legal & Political Mastery

University Law College & University of Balochistan. Graduated with honors, mastering the intricacies of law and governance, and laying the groundwork for a transformative legal career.

2025 - Legal License & Practice

Received Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025). Formally joined Iqra Law Associates under Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar, beginning a journey of professional excellence and social impact.
