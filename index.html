About Me

Professional Profile

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan, I am the eldest of nine siblings, son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon). My journey from humble beginnings in village schooling to achieving academic excellence through scholarships at Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges has been driven by discipline, determination, and a commitment to justice.

As a multilingual legal professional fluent in Pashto, Urdu, English, and Turkish, I bridge cultural and linguistic gaps in the legal arena. My practice is rooted in the belief that law should serve as an instrument of social change and empowerment, particularly for marginalized communities in Balochistan.

Beyond the courtroom, I serve as a Global Shaper with the World Economic Forum, curating the Quetta Hub and contributing to global conversations on governance, youth empowerment, and human rights. My work has taken me to international platforms including the Global Shapers Annual Summit 2025 in Geneva.
