About Me

Legal Visionary & Human Rights Advocate

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. As the eldest of nine siblings and son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon), my journey from village schooling to becoming an Advocate High Court is defined by discipline, brilliance, and unwavering commitment to justice.

From scholarship-based excellence at Pak-Turk Maarif to mastering the intricacies of law at University Law College, I have consistently demonstrated leadership and academic prowess. My multilingual fluency in Pashto, Urdu, English, and Turkish has enabled me to connect with diverse communities and advocate for their rights.

  • Advocate High Court with Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025)
  • Global Shaper at World Economic Forum, Curator of Quetta Hub
  • LLB (Hons) & MA Political Science Graduate with honors
  • Author of "Human Rights and Responsibilities" (2021)
  • Fluent in Pashto, Urdu, English, and Turkish
  • Active practitioner in Civil, Criminal, and Human Rights litigation

Educational Journey

2006 - 2018: School Education

Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges. Awarded scholarships for academic excellence throughout my schooling. Demonstrated leadership skills and multilingual fluency (Pashto, Urdu, English, Turkish).

2019: Intermediate (FSc. Pre-Engineering)

Completed with distinction from Pak-Turk Maarif International College. Developed strong analytical and problem-solving skills that would later serve my legal career.

2022: BA (Arts)

University of Balochistan. Building the foundation for social sciences and political thought. Actively participated in debate competitions and student politics.

2024: LLB (Hons) & MA Political Science

University Law College & University of Balochistan. Graduated with honors, mastering the intricacies of law and governance. Published research on "The Role of ECP in Free and Fair Elections" (2024).

2025: Legal License & Professional Practice

Received Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025). Formally joined Iqra Law Associates under Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar. Started practicing as an Advocate High Court.
