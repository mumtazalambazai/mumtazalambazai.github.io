The Journey of Excellence

From the valleys of Balochistan to global platforms, a journey defined by resilience, brilliance, and unwavering commitment to justice.

2000 - Birth & Early Life

Born in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. Eldest of nine siblings, son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon). Early education rooted in traditional village schooling.

2019 - Intermediate Excellence

Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges. Scholarship-based education. Developed multilingual fluency (Pashto, Urdu, English, Turkish) and leadership foundations.

2022 - BA (Arts) Foundation

University of Balochistan. Built strong foundation in social sciences and political thought, preparing for legal and policy-oriented career.

2024 - Dual Degrees

Graduated with LLB (Hons) from University Law College and MA Political Science from University of Balochistan. Mastered legal theory and governance principles.

2025 - Legal Practice

Received Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025). Joined Iqra Law Associates under Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar. Began professional legal practice.
