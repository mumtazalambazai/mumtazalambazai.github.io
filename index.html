The Journey

From Kili Zdoon to Global Platforms - A Story of Resilience and Excellence

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan

Mumtaz Alam Bazai is the eldest of nine siblings and son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad, a distinguished neurosurgeon. His journey from village schooling to becoming a Global Shaper is a testament to his discipline, brilliance, and unwavering commitment to justice.

Educated at Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges on scholarship, he demonstrated exceptional leadership and multilingual fluency in Pashto, Urdu, English, and Turkish.

His academic journey continued at the University of Balochistan where he earned his BA in Arts (2022), followed by an LLB (Hons) and MA in Political Science (2024), graduating with honors and mastering the intricacies of law and governance.
