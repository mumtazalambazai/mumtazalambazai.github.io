My Journey

From Balochistan to Global Platforms

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan, I am the eldest of nine siblings, son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon). My journey from village schooling to international platforms embodies resilience, academic excellence, and unwavering commitment to justice.

Educated at Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges on scholarship, I demonstrated early leadership and multilingual fluency (Pashto, Urdu, English, Turkish). This foundation propelled me through higher education at the University of Balochistan, where I earned BA (Arts), LLB (Hons), and MA (Political Science) with distinction.

In 2025, I received my Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025) and joined Iqra Law Associates under Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar, beginning my formal legal practice.
