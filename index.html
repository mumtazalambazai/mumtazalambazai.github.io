The Journey

From the villages of Balochistan to Global Stages - A Story of Resilience and Vision

Born on 11 August 2000 in Kili Zdoon, District Pishin, Balochistan. Eldest of nine siblings, son of Dr. Ameer Muhammad (Neurosurgeon). From humble beginnings to becoming a legal luminary and global advocate.

2019

Intermediate Education

Pak-Turk Maarif International Schools & Colleges. Excelled academically while developing multilingual fluency in Pashto, Urdu, English, and Turkish. Demonstrated early leadership capabilities.

2022

Bachelor of Arts

University of Balochistan. Built a strong foundation in social sciences, political theory, and governance, preparing for a career in law and public service.

2024

LLB (Hons) & MA Political Science

University Law College & University of Balochistan. Graduated with honors, mastering constitutional law, human rights, and governance systems. Authored research papers on electoral systems.

2025

Legal License & Professional Practice

Received Bar Council License (Reg No. 14/2025). Joined Iqra Law Associates under Advocate Abdul Zahir Kakar. Began practicing in Civil, Criminal, and Human Rights litigation.
